HARRIS HOUSE RECIPES

Get the recipe book on your iPhone

About 5 minutes, one time. There's nothing to download from the App Store. The book lives on the web, and you put an icon for it on your Home Screen. After that it works just like an app.

Before you start

  • 📱Your iPhone, on Wi-Fi or with signal.
  • 🧭Safari, the blue compass app. Other browsers won't do step 3.
  • 🙋Jordan, nearby. He has the setup code for Part 2.

Part 1

Put it on your Home Screen

Tip: read steps 2 to 4 before you start. If you're reading this on your iPhone, Safari's back arrow ‹ brings you back here.

  1. Open the book in Safari

    Open the book

    Or tap the link Jordan texts or AirDrops you. It opens in Safari.

    Or, if you're reading this on a computer or on paper: open your iPhone's Camera, point it at this square, and tap the link that pops up.

    No link? In Safari, type jgrayharris.github.io/recipe-book

    You're in the right place when you see HARRIS HOUSE RECIPES at the top and pictures of food.

  2. Tap the Share button

    It's a square with an arrow pointing up , at the bottom of the screen.

    Don't see it? Tap ••• at the bottom first, then Share.

  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    A list slides up. Scroll down it and tap Add to Home Screen. It has a square with a plus next to it.

  4. Tap Add

    Leave the name as Recipes. If there's an Open as Web App switch, leave it on. Tap Add at the top right.

    Then go to your Home Screen. The new icon is there, often on the last page:

    Recipes

The book is on your phone. From now on, open it from this icon, not from Safari. The icon remembers your settings and Safari doesn't.

Part 2 · with Jordan

Connect it

This lets your phone add recipes to the shared book, and lets Jordan see your favorites. Easiest of all: hand your phone to Jordan for this part.

  1. Tap your name

    Open Recipes from the new icon. Tap ⚙️ at the top right. Under Who's using this phone? tap Lauren.

    It says “Hi, Lauren!”. Your name now goes on the recipes you add.

  2. Paste the setup code

    Jordan sends you a long code that starts with github_pat_. Press and hold it, then tap Copy.

    Open Recipes again from the icon. On the same ⚙️ page, tap the box under Setup code, tap it again, tap Paste, then tap Save.

    The code is like a password for the book. Once it's saved, you can delete it from your messages. The app remembers it.

You'll see “All set: this phone can add recipes.”, and the page says the code is Saved.

Part 3 · to check it works

Save your first recipe

This is also how you'll save every recipe from now on.

  1. Copy a recipe's link

    • TikTokTap the Share arrow on the right of the video, then Copy link.
    • InstagramTap the paper airplane, then Copy link.
    • RedditTap Share, then Copy link.

  2. Open Recipes and tap Add

    Tap ＋ Add at the bottom, then the big red button.

    📖Book🧺What we have🛒List＋Add
    📋 Paste link and add

  3. Tap Paste

    A small Paste bubble pops up. Tap it.

    That's your iPhone checking it's OK for the app to use what you copied.

You'll see “Added your TikTok link 🍳”. You're all set up.

Where's my recipe?

New recipes show up in the book on the next Monday or Thursday afternoon. That's when the chef (the helper that turns videos into recipe cards) writes them up. Until then, yours waits at the bottom of the Add page, under In the kitchen.

Don't worry, you can't break it

If something goes wrong

“Add to Home Screen” isn't in the list

Check you're in Safari, the blue compass app, and not inside TikTok, Instagram or another app. Then scroll to the very bottom of the list, tap Edit Actions, and add it.

I can't find the icon

Swipe left to the last page of your Home Screen. Or swipe down in the middle of the Home Screen and type Recipes.

It says “Couldn't load the book”

Check you're on Wi-Fi or have signal, then tap Try again.

The ⚙️ page still says “Not set yet”

The code didn't get saved. Copy it again, paste it in the box, and tap Save. Make sure you opened Recipes from the icon, not from Safari.

“That didn't work: the setup code is wrong or expired”

Part of the code may be missing. Ask Jordan to send it again. If it still doesn't work, he'll make a new one.

“Set up this phone first”, or the red button is grey

Part 2 isn't finished. Go back to step 6.

“No link on the clipboard”

The link didn't get copied. Go back to step 7 and tap Copy link again.

“Couldn't read the clipboard”

Tap the big box under or paste a list, tap Paste, then tap Add these to the book.

I did Part 2 in Safari by mistake

No harm done. Open Recipes from the icon and do Part 2 again there.

Stuck?

Text Jordan. And if a recipe link is all you have, sending it to him works too.
